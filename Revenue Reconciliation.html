Revenue Reconciliation

Finance's monthly book, built from the fee tool. Give every line a status, download the flash, lock the month. A locked month never changes — and a project edit that moves one shows up red here until you reopen it.

Viewing as
How this works.   Each project has up to three lines a month — Fee (what the client is billed for the fee, including the fee on any pass-through), Pass-through (vendor cost billed through Savills) and Fee share (the broker or co-party cut, a minus). Leaders own earned: the month the work happened, in their project. Finance owns the billing layer here: billed as planned, billed a different amount, accrued to a later month, slipped to a later month, or written off. An accrual carries into every following month until it is billed; the month it bills shows the amount billed and a minus against accruals. A different amount or a slip puts a flag on the project for its leader to fix — nothing here rewrites their figures. Lock freezes the month: nothing changes here afterwards, and any project edit that moves a locked figure is flagged in red at the top until an admin reopens the month.
