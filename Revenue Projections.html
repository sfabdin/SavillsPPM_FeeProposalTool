How this is built.   Every project's monthly invoice is computed from its saved fee record — respecting each project's billing mode (by-phase or flatlined), discount, escalation and Rate Lock — and laid out by calendar month. Rows are sorted by the 1–7 projection rating. Ratings 1–4 (Booked through 50–74%) are included in the monthly totals at full invoice value; ratings 5–7 are shown greyed and excluded from the totals. The probability-weighted line applies each rating's confidence (95/82/62/37/15%) to every project — a more conservative forecast. This is the first cut of the Phase 5 projections engine: one number, born in the fee matrix, flowing straight to the forecast.

Vocabulary.   The monthly figure here is EARNED revenue — the month the work is performed, the month staffing is tied to. When revenue is realized (accrued) and when the invoice actually goes out are separate calendars, assigned per month on Revenue Reconciliation: ACCRUED is the month revenue is recognized; INVOICED is the month it bills out the door.
