Monthly Confirmed Book

Once a month, every revenue leader confirms their book. The confirmed book is what leadership reports on and what next month is compared against.

Viewing as
How this works.   Confirming copies every project you lead into this month's confirmed book with your name and the time on each copy. One confirmation per month — it is locked in. If a project changes afterwards the tracker says so, and the change waits for next month. A project shared between two leaders is fully confirmed once both have confirmed; the most recent confirmation supplies the copy. When an admin locks the month, anyone who has not confirmed has their live projects carried in and flagged, and can still confirm afterwards with a reason.
