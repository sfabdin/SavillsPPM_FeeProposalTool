How this works.   The matrix in Section 03 maps each role’s FTE % across eight project periods. The cost engine multiplies FTE % × period months × monthly rate for every role / period combination; 2028 periods use the escalated rate. The grand total is the sum of all role-period subtotals, less the volume discount applied at the total-fee level.

Parity with the proposal deck.   Period totals here feed slides 19–21 of Proposal for 383M.html. PIC / Advisory is set to no charge by default, consistent with the standing JPMC relationship.

Editable.   Every yellow cell — rates, FTE %, hours / mo, escalation, discount — is an input. Export CSV to share assumptions with JPMC procurement, or print to PDF for a one-page fee summary.
