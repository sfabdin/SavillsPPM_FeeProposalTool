Financial Tracking (TEST)

Finance's working sheet, one month of work performed at a time: the revenue leader's earned figure locked, then prior EOM, Flash, Final and EOM side by side with the deltas, the billing with its invoice and date, and what is accrued but not yet billed. Take the three snapshots from the stepper as the billing month runs. The original Financial Tracking page is untouched; this one keeps its own records.

Viewing as
How this works.   Earned is the revenue leader's number from Revenue Projections and is never edited here. Accrued is what Finance recognises for the month, with its own month and amount; Billed is the invoice, with its own month, amount, number and date. The two can differ on purpose. Status follows those lanes: not yet billed, awaiting invoice approval, discuss with CR, accrued · bill TBD (a hold), billed. The EOM column is the working accrual; Flash, Final and EOM are frozen copies of it taken from the stepper, and last month's EOM snapshot is this month's Prior EOM. Fee shares are their own negative rows with the broker named. Export EOM file writes the sheet in Finance's layout. The Unbilled schedule view is derived from the same lines: an opening balance typed once from last year's schedule, then each month's accruals less billings to a running balance per line, with fee shares carried as negatives; Export schedule writes it in the schedule's layout.
