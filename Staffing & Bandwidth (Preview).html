What the words mean.

The same definitions the live page uses.
Contract
What the signed (or proposed) fee record priced: titles and FTE by month, no names. Edited in the calculator.
Plan
Who we intend to staff: named people with an allocation percentage by month, entered on the live Staffing page.
Actual
What Clockify says was logged, placed on the fee project through the mapping.
FTE
One full-time person for one month. Capacity is the person's own month: a part-timer at 60% is 0.6 FTE.
Load
A person's allocation percentages added across every project in a month. Over 100% is over the line.
Pursuit
A proposal not yet won. Its staffing is shown hatched and only counts when you ask "what if we win".
Weighted demand
Pursuit staffing multiplied by the project's rating weight (90%+ counts 0.95, under 25% counts 0.15).
Finished
Closed out, lost, or the last month of the timeline has passed. Finished work is never chased.
